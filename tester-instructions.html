While on your Android phone, please follow these steps:

  1. Join the testers Google Group by clicking this link: Shopping Wizard Testers google group. (Then click "Join Group")
  2. Sign up to test the app by clicking this link: Sign up as a tester. (Then click "Become a tester", followed by "download it on Google Play", and then "Install")
  3. Afer downloading the app, please run it to see if it works!
  4. Please don't leave the testing group or uninstall the app for a month. If you bail out too soon, Google won't count you as a tester.
